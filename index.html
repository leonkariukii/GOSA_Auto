About Garage_plus

Garage_plus is a SaaS platform designed to help garages organize their operations and provide better service to their customers.

Our goal is to make garage management simpler, more organized and more transparent.

Our Features

Job Card Management

Create and manage digital job cards for vehicles entering the workshop.

Customer Management

Keep customer and vehicle information organized in one place.

Service History

Keep track of previous vehicle services and repairs.

How Garage_plus Works

  1. Register the Customer

    Record the customer's details and vehicle information.

  2. Create a Job Card

    Create a job card describing the vehicle's service or repair requirements.

  3. Complete the Service

    Track the work and keep the service record for future reference.
